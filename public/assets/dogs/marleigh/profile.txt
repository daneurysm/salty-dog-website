Meet Marleigh—a big, affectionate girl who has been waiting far too long for her home.

Marleigh came to Salty Dog last May frightened and underweight after being abandoned. Today, she is healthy, happy and eager to make friends. She loves people, gets along well with the other shelter dogs and enjoys long leash walks, forest outings and running alongside a bicycle.

Marleigh turns 3 in November. She is spayed, vaccinated, microchipped, house-trained and an excellent passenger in the car.

Because she is large, strong and sometimes overly excited by bicycles, skateboards, deer and unfamiliar dogs, Marleigh would thrive in a calm home with someone experienced with strong dogs who can provide structure and continue working with her.
